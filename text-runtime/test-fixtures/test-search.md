# Quantum Computing Basics

Quantum computing leverages **superposition** and **entanglement** to perform computations.

## Qubits

A qubit is the fundamental unit of quantum information. Unlike classical bits which are always 0 or 1,
qubits can exist in a **superposition** of states.

## Quantum Gates

Quantum gates manipulate qubits through unitary transformations. The Hadamard gate creates superposition,
while the CNOT gate creates entanglement between qubits.

## Applications

Quantum computers could revolutionize cryptography, drug discovery, and optimization problems.
